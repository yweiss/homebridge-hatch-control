# Hatch Control 0.3.0

Initial release of Hatch Control by Yoav Weiss.

- Receiver controls for Rest 2, Rest+ and Rest Mini, including named sounds, volume and supported light controls.
- Remembered playback settings and No Sound at the end of the sound picker.
- Experimental routine selection and power controls for Restore and Hatch Baby.

Rest 2nd Gen is hardware-tested. Additional models have automated test coverage and await hardware validation. All 52 tests pass on Node 22, 24 and 26.

See the [setup guide](https://github.com/yweiss/homebridge-hatch-control#readme) for installation and compatibility. Credits retained for Dusty Greif, Scott MacWatters and Brian Via. MIT licensed.
